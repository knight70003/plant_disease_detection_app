Questions

How do I read the confidence score?

It shows how strongly the model favors its top answer. If the score is low or a second match is close, retake the photo in better light or ask a local agronomist.

Where do the treatment suggestions come from?

They are generated by a language model from the detected disease name. Treat them as a starting point and confirm them with local agronomy guidance.

Are my photos saved?

Yes. Each scanned image is stored on the server with its result so it can appear in your dashboard and history.

Which crops are supported?

Corn, grape, potato and tomato, covering the ten conditions listed above.
